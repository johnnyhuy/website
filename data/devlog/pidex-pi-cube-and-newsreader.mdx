I put the Pi mark back on Pidex's isometric cube and worked through the variants
the app actually needs: mono, inverse, stacked and icon treatments that hold up
on the dark canvas.[^change]

It was a small identity pass, but it crossed more than one surface. The change
included the app's mark component, the cube artwork and a brand demo board, rather
than leaving the logo as a standalone image that the interface did not use.

Newsreader came in as bundled body type, with Georgia as the fallback. That gave
the text side of the app a deliberate pairing with the geometric mark instead of
trying to make the cube carry the whole identity.

## Check the mark where it lives

The useful part of making a demo board was seeing the family together. A mark
that reads well by itself still needs to survive as a small icon, an empty-state
element and an inverse treatment.

The result is a set of usable pieces rather than one finished-looking logo.
The cube, small mark and body type can now be checked together in the interface.

[^change]: [Pidex's Pi cube, mark variants and Newsreader change](https://github.com/echohello-dev/pidex/pull/22).
