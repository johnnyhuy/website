I rebuilt this site around the blog. The bento homepage gave way to a flatter
industrial layout: hairline borders, sharp corners, mono labels and one yellow
accent. The introduction became smaller, with the writing list doing more of
the work.[^redesign]

I changed the title to Builder and removed the activity and presence widgets.
The experience timeline no longer needed to compete with the posts on the home
page either. Eighteen dead components came out in the same pass.

## Keep the old versions somewhere

I added an archive for the 2020 Jekyll site, the first Next.js cut and the bento
version. They are separate saved builds served under `/archive`, rather than
old layouts reconstructed inside the current component tree.

![A blog-row detail from the archived bento-era website, with a rounded panel, date, summary and outlined tags](/images/devlog/website-archive-blog-list.webp)

_A blog-row detail in the archived bento-era site. Relative dates reflect this later screenshot, not the redesign day._

That kept the earlier work viewable without making the new site carry all three
designs. I also added robots rules for the archived copies.

The redesign included lint-tooling repair as well: the old `next lint` path no
longer fitted Next 16. The changed files were checked, while existing errors in
untouched UI files remained separate work.

[^redesign]: [The blog-first redesign and version archive](https://github.com/johnnyhuy/website/pull/9).
