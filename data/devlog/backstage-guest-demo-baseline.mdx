My public [Backstage showcase](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage)
spent the weekend collecting polish: a plugin showcase page, deep links into
the scaffolder. The quiet fix was in the auth story. A guest demo baseline is
only real when the catalog, the permission policy and the app config agree on
who the guest is
([PR #111](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/pull/111),
[closing #110](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/issues/110)).

The guest user now actually exists in the catalog as `user:default/guest`, a
member of `readers`, alongside the `readers`, `developers` and `admins` groups
the permission policy names. The guest reference in `app-config.yaml` is
explicit to match production, and the baseline is recorded in
`docs/demo-baseline.md` with read-only tests that keep it honest. Before this
change the guest existed in the demo story, not in the catalog.

The showcase work around it is straightforward. The new `/showcase` page
curates what the portal already ships: software templates, GitHub integration
cards and answer engine cards, with search and category filters
([PR #108](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/pull/108),
[closing #107](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/issues/107)).
Showcase template cards now deep-link to their own scaffolder pages through
the real Template entity names instead of dropping visitors on the generic
`/self-service` list
([PR #113](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/pull/113),
[closing #112](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/issues/112)).

One fix is still open. The catalog defined the same groups and guest user
twice across its example sources, and the open change makes `examples/` the
single source for those definitions and deletes the duplicates
([PR #115](https://github.com/echohello-dev/backstage/pull/115)). That is the
generalisable half: demo and test baselines have to live in the same catalog
the policy reads, and every catalog definition wants exactly one owner.
